Heavy music, pressed to vinyl

Metal has always been more than distortion and double kick drums — it's a family tree with roots in blues and prog rock, branching into thrash, death, doom, and everything in between. Metalcore fused that aggression with hardcore punk's breakdowns and melody; punk stripped everything back down to three chords and a reason to shout them.

BlackVinyl exists for people who still want a physical copy: real cover art, real liner notes, and a shelf that tells a story. Browse by genre below, dig into a band's full discography, or search for that one record you've been meaning to pick up.
